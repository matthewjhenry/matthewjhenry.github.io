Interactive charts of daily maximum temperature, built with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code). Each point is one day's high temperature; hover a point to see its exact date and value, and type a year into the box in the top-right to highlight every day from that year.

* [Grenoble–St Geoirs, France](/files/visualisations/grenoble_daily_high_interactive.html) — daily high temperature since 1950, from [Météo-France](https://meteo.data.gouv.fr) open data.
* [Leeds / Bradford, United Kingdom](/files/visualisations/leeds_daily_high_interactive.html) — daily high temperature since 1908, from Met Office [MIDAS Open](https://catalogue.ceda.ac.uk/uuid/dbd451271eb04662beade68da43546e1/) data via the CEDA Archive.
